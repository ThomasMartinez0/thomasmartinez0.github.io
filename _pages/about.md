I am a 5th year Math PhD Student at UCLA's Math department working with [Pavel Galashin.](https://www.math.ucla.edu/~galashin/) I am primarily interested in algebraic and enumerative combinatorics. I am currently working on problems related to the combinatorics of open positroid varieties, their various indexing objects, and their relationship to Catalan combinatorics. This includes Deodhar diagrams, Richardson links, and plabic graphs. Before UCLA, I received my B.S. with High Distinction and Honors in Mathematics at Harvey Mudd College in 2021.

I am currently on the job market.
