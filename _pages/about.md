<div class="home-intro">
  <p>I am a mathematics PhD candidate at UCLA, working with <a href="https://www.math.ucla.edu/~galashin/">Pavel Galashin</a>. I am interested in algebraic and enumerative combinatorics, particularly the combinatorics and geometry of positroid varieties. My work involves affine Deodhar diagrams, Richardson links, plabic graphs, and Catalan combinatorics.</p>

  <p>Before UCLA, I earned a B.S. in mathematics with High Distinction and Honors from Harvey Mudd College in 2021. Outside of mathematics, I enjoy <a href="https://letterboxd.com/rottnTM/">movies</a> and taking <a href="https://www.flickr.com/people/thomasmart/">photos</a>, primarily on film.</p>

  <p>I am on the academic job market and expect to complete my PhD in 2027.</p>
