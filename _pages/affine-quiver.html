Reading the explorer

Enter the words

n counts the nodes of the affine Dynkin diagram of type An−1(1). Enter generators 1 through n. Both n and 0 denote s0. Spaces or commas separate letters. For n ≤ 9, compact words such as 123123 also work. Use e or an empty field for the identity. The explorer accepts 3–24 nodes and up to 48 letters per word.

The target v must be reduced and feasible in β. Positive β enables both constructions, including nonreduced braids. Negative letters in β switch to the signed plabic diagram.

Follow a disk

A solid bridge at word position d gives one quiver vertex and one soap film. Select it on the diagram or in the film menu. Choose Trace from birth to start at cut d−1, then step left to build the film one crossing at a time. A cut numbered c lies after the first c letters. The film is frozen when it reaches cut 0.

Top and Bottom show the two projections. Lifts shows integer heights before reducing modulo n. Several heights can cover the same face, so a film can contribute more than once. The 3D view shows the periodic strand diagram schematically, with film coverage on the two coordinate walls.

Step through Bao–Ye

Build opens the final quiver. Use the first-step arrow to return to the initial seed, then advance through individual mutations, freezes, and deletions. At the initial seed and after each deletion, the explorer computes the plabic quiver independently for that stage’s partial target. During mutations and freezes, the diagram shows the preceding completed stage.

The paper reads the reversed words and chooses a leftmost subexpression. The plabic diagram reads the entered words using a rightmost subexpression. Arrow directions are reversed once to align conventions. After each deletion, native vertex (i,k) corresponds to the kth omitted i in reversed β, reflected back to its original word position.

Match checks the same word positions, frozen vertices and weighted arrows incident to a mutable vertex. On the paper view, Frozen edges also shows the frozen–frozen entries, including halves, retained during mutation. Agreement is checked for the displayed input and stage.
